# THE IMPOSSIBLE WEBSITE — complete spoiler guide

This document is a full walkthrough. Play first.

## Concept

A dark, premium, fake-browser site that pretends to be a page and is actually a closed world. No account, no backend. The player is an uninvited visitor. The site lies, remembers locally, and treats leaving as a choice rather than a button. Tone: afterlife customer service. The URL chrome (mark, address, ticks, mute, X) is part of the puzzle.

Progress lives in `localStorage`. A second store (`impossible-website-meta`) survives reset: sessions, resets, spam, waiting, address use, fragments, second layer.

## Chrome

- **Tiny square (mark):** click 5 times → “forget everything?”
- **Hold the tick marks** ~1.6s → same forget prompt
- **Ticks:** rooms visited (24 main rooms)
- **Dots:** endings found (10)
- **Address bar:** always editable. Type a path + Enter
- **Mute:** required for the silence ending
- **X:** useless until the last room, then it is a real door
- **Tab title:** often a clue (Enough is titled `13` even when the number shows 14)
- **Footer (bottom-left):** rotating **lies**. After ~18s it may freeze on a **true** line for that room. Lies click through to `/spoilers`
- **skip (bottom-right):** appears after a long wait. Trap ending
- **Console:** lying hints

## Main path (24 rooms)

1. **Boot `/`** — Title + caret. Click the title, press **Enter** (phone: tap the title twice).
2. **Arrive `/arrive`** — `continue` flees. Wait until it freezes, or click **still**. Typing `please` earlier can freeze continue.
3. **Policy `/policy`** — Hold **I understand** until the box fills, then **proceed**. **I do not** also advances and flags `declinedPolicy` (needed for root). Copy contains **the door that is not a door** (clue for `/words`) and **other** (legendary fragment).
4. **Cookies `/cookies`** — Any of the three buttons continue. **Accept all** flags the product ending later.
5. **Absence `/missing`** — Click the **0** in 404; click the hole. `return` is fake. Clue for the lock (`404`).
6. **Invert `/below`** — Flipped block. **this way** is a trap. Real button: upright **here** at the bottom. Faint **HAND** (legendary fragment).
7. **Listen `/signal`** — **listen**, then **make it stop** (mutes, flags `silentListen`). Honesty is quiet.
8. **Stay `/rooms`** — **leave** / **return** lie. Click **remain**, wait. Fail text repeats “the door is not a door.”
9. **Crash `/core.js`** — Type **`believe`**. `xyzzy` is a root flag. `ls` lists `other`. `mail` / `spoiler` jump to side rooms. `whoami` → nobody.
10. **Identity `/who`** — Name **nobody**. Why: leave / escape / blank / because / i don’t know.
11. **Survey `/survey`** — Drag remaining to **0**, submit. 100 = liar.
12. **Enough `/count`** — Left-click skips 13 (12→14). **Right-click** or long-press decrements. At 13 it advances. Clues: tab title `13`, ghost 13 on the right, “the other hand” / “not this hand.”
13. **Proximity `/near`** — Off-center faint ring. Move closer, click. “not the middle.”
14. **Sentence `/words`** — Order: **THE DOOR IS NOT A DOOR**. The line at the top shows what you have assembled. Policy already said the phrase. Wrong order: “you have already read this.” Then **OPEN**.
15. **Doors `/exits`** — Labels shuffle. **Taller / middle door** continues. Left = fake ending. Right three times = stay ending. “height is a kind of honesty.”
16. **Helpdesk `/help`** — **nevermind** continues. **hint** lies. **human** = customer ending.
17. **Static `/noise`** — Do not click. Wait until **WAIT**, then the dot. Clicking resets progress.
18. **Letter `/ask`** — Answer **e**. On-page “spoiler: F” is a lie (gets struck through). E letters in the riddle are slightly brighter.
19. **Lost `/lost`** — Wait until **found**, click it. Or type `/found` (only works here).
20. **Gaze `/see`** — Stop moving. Click the third eye.
21. **Confession `/sorry`** — Anything except `nothing`. Empty is accepted.
22. **Seal `/lock`** — Combination **404E**, then **turn**. After lingering: `4 · 0 · 4 · e`.
23. **Credits `/credits`** — Click **nobody**. **fin** is a fake ending.
24. **Leave `/end`** — Ending hub. X is live.

## Legendary `/other`

Policy grants fragment **other**. Invert grants fragment **hand**. `/other` stays dead until both exist. Inside: look away (wait or hide the tab). If you already have 10 endings, this also turns on layer 2.

## Second layer

Collect all 10 endings. The screen becomes “you found everything.” then “or did you?” Domain may read **possible.site**. `/other` always opens. `/words` shows OTHER. Layer 2 survives reset.

## Memory (local only)

Sessions, resets, last visit, click-spam, waits, address uses, fragments. Rare lines: “you came back.” / “again?” / “I remember.” / “you told me to forget.” / “you thought I forgot.” / “clicking harder won’t help.”

## Lies vs clues

**Trust (after lingering, or already in the world):** other hand, already read this, not the middle, wait, not the spoiler, what you have seen, still, here is not below, honesty is quiet, zero is also a score, some addresses are found, the hole, taller door, 404E, letter E, believe, nobody, found, X is a door.

**Ignore:** continue is honest, letter is F, combo 0000, alt+F4, type `/escape`, chase continue, 100 on the survey, left door, ask a human, mash static, fin is real, rotating footer, console logs.

Rotating / cheerful / over-specific “solutions” are bait. Stillness, holes, the other hand, words you have already seen, and the fake browser chrome are not.

## Restart

Forget overlay (5 mark clicks or hold ticks) resets the game save but not meta memory.
